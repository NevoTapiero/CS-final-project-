About us!

We're just a group of teens with a very relatable and big problem that sadly no one talks about, which is finding a movie to watch! This problem gave us the idea for our website "MovieToSee". We; Galia Sulpovar, Mayar Sinnokrot, Asil Gazmawi and Nevo Tapiero, came up with this idea after hours and hours of thinking. With MovieToSee, you can find the best movies of each genre, actors and years! Our webiste is easy to use and useful, so enjoy using it!
